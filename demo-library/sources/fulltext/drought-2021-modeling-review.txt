TITLE: Challenges in modeling and predicting floods and droughts: A review
COVERAGE: Abstract excerpt from Crossref; not full text.

ABSTRACT EXCERPT
In this review, we (a) identify challenges common to drought and flood prediction and their joint assessment and (b) discuss tractable approaches to tackle these challenges. We group challenges related to flood and drought prediction into four interrelated categories: data, process understanding, modeling and prediction, and human–water interactions. Data‐related challenges include data availability and event definition. Process‐related challenges include the multivariate and spatial characteristics of extremes, non‐stationarities, and future changes in extremes.
