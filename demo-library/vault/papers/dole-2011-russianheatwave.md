# Was there a basis for anticipating the 2010 Russian heat wave?

## Summary

利用强迫模式模拟与观测，检验能否基于自然与人为强迫提前预警 2010 年俄罗斯热浪：结论是这次热浪主要由持久阻塞事件背后的大气内部动力过程造成，缓变边界条件（海温、海冰、温室气体）没有起到明显作用。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [blocking, persistent-circulation-anomaly, forced-vs-internal-variability]: "Analysis of observations indicate that this heat wave was mainly due to internal atmospheric dynamical processes that produced and maintained a strong and long-lived blocking event, and that similar atmospheric patterns have occurred with prior heat waves in this region." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [forced-vs-internal-variability, extreme-event-linkage]: "We conclude that the intense 2010 Russian heat wave was mainly due to natural internal atmospheric variability." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C3 [forced-vs-internal-variability]: "Slowly varying boundary conditions that could have provided predictability and the potential for early warning did not appear to play an appreciable role in this event." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
