# Quasi-resonant circulation regimes and hemispheric synchronization of extreme weather in boreal summer

## Summary

基于对流层中层风场的谱分析，表明近年的持续急流型主要来自 6–8 波准静止波的放大，同时伴随纬向平均急流减弱；异常环流型导致持续的地面天气，使中纬度热浪与强降水在月尺度上同步。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [planetary-waves, persistent-circulation-anomaly]: "Many of these events were associated with anomalous jet stream circulation patterns characterized by persistent high-amplitude quasi-stationary Rossby waves." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [planetary-waves, persistent-circulation-anomaly]: "Based upon spectral analysis of the midtroposphere wind field, we show that the persistent jet stream patterns were, in the first place, due to an amplification of quasi-stationary waves with zonal wave numbers 6-8." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C3 [persistent-circulation-anomaly, extreme-event-linkage]: "Furthermore, we demonstrate that the anomalous circulation regimes lead to persistent surface weather conditions and therefore to midlatitude synchronization of extreme heat and rainfall events on monthly timescales." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
