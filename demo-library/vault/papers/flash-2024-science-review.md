# Flash drought: A state of the science review

## Summary

本文是关于骤旱（flash drought）研究现状的综述，核心问题是：自 2002 年该术语出现以来，学界如何界定、监测、归因和预测骤旱，以及关键缺口在哪里。方法上，作者在 Web of Science 以 “flash drought” 检索并筛选出截至 2023 年 8 月、至少包含一项骤旱分析的 138 篇论文，按定义框架、监测指标、气候态特征、大气与海洋驱动、次季节—季节（S2S）预报、气候变化预估、社会科学与级联影响逐项梳理。综述主张以“快速发生—干旱发展—产生影响”三元组作为共同基础，而不是追求统一定义，并强调定义与指标的选择会显著改变热点、趋势与归因结论。主要结论包括：萨赫勒与印度是最一致的热点区；S2S 预报技巧多限于 1–3 周，统计/机器学习方法可延长至约 4 周且以陆面初始状态贡献最大；多数历史与预估研究显示骤旱频率上升，但区域、模式与指标之间分歧很大。边界：这是叙述性综述，未产生新数据；对影响与社会科学的实证证据薄弱，且缓存版本为作者接受稿，页码与最终出版版不同。

## Limitations & gaps

- Review is a narrative synthesis of published work; the paper states no new data were created or analysed, so no claim here rests on new empirical analysis.
- Definitional heterogeneity is treated as an irreducible feature: attributions, hot spot mapping and trend estimates remain sensitive to the chosen variable and threshold (C4), and no indicator intercomparison yet exists (C30).
- Temperature-based demand estimates (Thornthwaite-type) can overstate flash drought frequency, while fully physical Penman-Monteith formulations reduce that bias (C10, C12 context) — a source of disagreement across indicator-based studies.
- Satellite soil moisture sees only the top centimetres, and land surface models omit irrigation and groundwater pumping (C13, C14), so root-zone and human-modified drought signals are weakly observed.
- Forecast skill is short and event-dependent, with some events (e.g., the 2017 U.S. High Plains flash drought) showing no S2S skill in the dynamical systems reviewed (C20).
- Projection uncertainty is unresolved across regions, models and metrics (C23); social-science and impact evidence remains limited and U.S.-agriculture focused (C24).
