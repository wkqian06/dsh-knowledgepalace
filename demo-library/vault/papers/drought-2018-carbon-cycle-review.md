# Drought, Heat, and the Carbon Cycle: a Review

## Summary

本文系统综述干旱与高温事件（DHE）如何影响陆地碳循环，核心问题是这些极端事件通过哪些生态生理与物候过程起作用、受哪些生态系统属性与时间结构调节、又如何与更大尺度的大气环流相联系。方法上是叙述性综述：以 Web of Science 检索（2017 年 10 月，2018 年 2 月更新）获得的 1040 篇文献为基础人工筛选分类，并综合通量观测、控制实验与遥感证据。主要结论是 DHE 通过 VPD 升高与气孔关闭、光合降幅大于呼吸等机制抑制碳吸收，其影响依事件的时间、持续时间与前期状态而放大或衰减，并经遗留效应、补偿/放大机制以及 ENSO、NAO 等环流型在区域至全球尺度上累积；2003 年欧洲事件的约 0.5 PgC 损失相当于 4 年陆地净碳吸收。边界在于事件样本有限、极端事件定义不统一、通量站点覆盖不均、遥感对森林生理响应不敏感、模型对生物过程刻画偏经验化，因此区域尺度影响的定量估计与归因仍不确定。

## Limitations & gaps

- Synthesis is constrained by the small in-situ sample of extreme events and by methods spanning plot experiments to global modelling (C11).
- No universally accepted definition, metric or index of C-cycle-relevant extremes; results depend on drought-vs-heatwave framing and baseline choice (C9, C5).
- Twenty-first-century drought projections remain widely uncertain even though some regions (e.g. Southern Europe) show confident trends (C8).
- Manipulation experiments confound soil moisture with VPD and use step changes, biasing mechanism attribution (C35).
- Remote sensing of canopy greenness misses physiological drought responses in forests; flux coverage remains incomplete and energy-balance closure is imperfect (C36).
- Land-surface models emphasise physical processes while biological processes (allocation, phenology, traits) are modelled ad hoc or empirically (C38); modelled drought responses may therefore be biased.
- Compensatory and amplifying dynamics (pre-event state, spatial compensation, soil moisture, boundary layer, albedo, mortality) are described but not systematically quantified (C32, C33).
- Effects of lateral moisture transport and land-use change on regional water recycling and hence on the C cycle are not assessed globally (C31).
- Narrative (non-meta-analytic) synthesis: no pooled effect sizes or formal uncertainty intervals are reported, which limits quantitative transfer of the reviewed mechanisms.
- Indirect pathways (fire, insect outbreaks, pathogens, litter/microbial change) are explicitly delegated to other literature rather than reviewed in depth (C6).
