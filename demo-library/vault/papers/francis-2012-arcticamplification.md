# Evidence linking Arctic amplification to extreme weather in mid‐latitudes

## Summary

分析 500 hPa 高度的逐日再分析资料，认为北极增暖放大使罗斯贝波东移变慢（纬向风减弱与波幅增大），从而使中纬度天气型更持久，可能增加干旱、洪涝、寒潮与热浪等持续性极端事件的概率。该结论是对一种可能性的论证；后续研究对其趋势有异议（见 barnes-2013-arcticrevisited）。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [planetary-waves]: "Two effects are identified that each contribute to a slower eastward progression of Rossby waves in the upper‐level flow: 1) weakened zonal winds, and 2) increased wave amplitude." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [persistent-circulation-anomaly, extreme-event-linkage]: "Slower progression of upper‐level waves would cause associated weather patterns in mid‐latitudes to be more persistent, which may lead to an increased probability of extreme weather events that result from prolonged conditions, such as drought, flooding, cold spells, and heat waves." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
