# Decadal Trends in the North Atlantic Oscillation: Regional Temperatures and Precipitation

## Summary

北大西洋涛动（NAO）近十年冬季持续处于同一极端位相，对欧洲冬季偏暖与西北大西洋偏冷有显著贡献；大气水汽收支显示 1980 年以来的大尺度变化与南欧、地中海干燥及北欧偏湿相关。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [modes-of-variability]: "Over the past decade, the Oscillation has remained in one extreme phase during the winters, contributing significantly to the recent wintertime warmth across Europe and to cold conditions in the northwest Atlantic." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [modes-of-variability, moisture-transport, drought-research]: "An evaluation of the atmospheric moisture budget reveals coherent large-scale changes since 1980 that are linked to recent dry conditions over southern Europe and the Mediterranean, whereas northern Europe and parts of Scandinavia have generally experienced wetter than normal conditions." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
