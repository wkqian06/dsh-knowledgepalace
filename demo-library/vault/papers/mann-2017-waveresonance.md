# Influence of Anthropogenic Climate Change on Planetary Wave Resonance and Extreme Weather Events

## Summary

从纬向平均地表温度剖面中分离出与准共振放大（QRA）有利条件相关的指纹；CMIP5 历史模拟在人为强迫下该指纹投影增强，多套观测资料中也有同样增强，且信号近期才从自然变率背景中显现。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [planetary-waves, persistent-circulation-anomaly]: "Persistent episodes of extreme weather in the Northern Hemisphere summer have been shown to be associated with the presence of high-amplitude quasi-stationary atmospheric Rossby waves within a particular wavelength range (zonal wavenumber 6-8)." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [planetary-waves, circulation-response-to-warming]: "Here, we isolate a specific fingerprint in the zonal mean surface temperature profile that is associated with QRA-favorable conditions." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
