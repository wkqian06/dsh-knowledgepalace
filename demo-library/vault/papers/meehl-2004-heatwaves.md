# More Intense, More Frequent, and Longer Lasting Heat Waves in the 21st Century

## Summary

全球耦合气候模式显示未来热浪有明显的地理格局：欧洲与北美（芝加哥 1995、巴黎 2003 型热浪）未来会更强、更频繁、持续更久；观测与模式表明当前热浪与一种特定的大气环流型同现，而该环流型被温室气体增加所增强。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [circulation-response-to-warming, extreme-event-linkage]: "Model results for areas of Europe and North America, associated with the severe heat waves in Chicago in 1995 and Paris in 2003, show that future heat waves in these areas will become more intense, more frequent, and longer lasting in the second half of the 21st century." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [persistent-circulation-anomaly, circulation-response-to-warming, extreme-event-linkage]: "Observations and the model show that present-day heat waves over Europe and North America coincide with a specific atmospheric circulation pattern that is intensified by ongoing increases in greenhouse gases, indicating that it will produce more severe heat waves in those regions in the future." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
