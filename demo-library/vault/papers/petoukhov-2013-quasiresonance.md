# Quasiresonant amplification of planetary waves and recent Northern Hemisphere weather extremes

## Summary

针对近年北半球夏季的多起极端事件（2003 欧洲热浪、2010 俄罗斯热浪与巴基斯坦洪水、2011 美国热浪），提出统一机制：中纬度波导内自由天气尺度波被捕获并准共振放大，形成 6–8 波的持续大振幅环流型。仅读到摘要，理论与再分析诊断细节需读全文。

## Claims

- C1 [planetary-waves, persistent-circulation-anomaly, extreme-event-linkage]: "Here, we propose a common mechanism for the generation of persistent longitudinal planetary-scale high-amplitude patterns of the atmospheric circulation in the Northern Hemisphere midlatitudes." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [planetary-waves]: "We show that these patterns might result from trapping within midlatitude waveguides of free synoptic waves with zonal wave numbers k ≈ m." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
