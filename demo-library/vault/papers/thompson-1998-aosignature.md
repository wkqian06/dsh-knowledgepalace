# The Arctic oscillation signature in the wintertime geopotential height and temperature fields

## Summary

提出“北极涛动”（AO）：冬季海平面气压第一经验正交函数，与欧亚大陆气温波动的耦合比 NAO 更强，可视为平流层极涡强度调制的地面印记。过去 30 年冬春季气温、海平面气压与 50 hPa 高度的趋势呈同样的模态结构，被解读为大气自然模态出现了系统性偏向。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [modes-of-variability]: "The leading empirical orthogonal function of the wintertime sea‐level pressure field is more strongly coupled to surface air temperature fluctuations over the Eurasian continent than the North Atlantic Oscillation (NAO)." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [modes-of-variability]: "Coupled to strong fluctuations at the 50‐hPa level on the intraseasonal, interannual, and interdecadal time scales, this “Arctic Oscillation” (AO) can be interpreted as the surface signature of modulations in the strength of the polar vortex aloft." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C3 [modes-of-variability, forced-vs-internal-variability]: "These trends can be interpreted as the development of a systematic bias in one of the atmosphere's dominant, naturally occurring modes of variability." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
