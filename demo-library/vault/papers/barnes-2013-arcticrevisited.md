# Revisiting the evidence linking Arctic amplification to extreme weather in midlatitudes

## Summary

在三套再分析中重新检验北极放大使行星波拉长、减速并增加阻塞的说法，认为此前报告的波幅正趋势很可能是方法造成的假象，阻塞频率在任何季节都没有显著增加。该文是对 francis-2012-arcticamplification 一类结论的反证。仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [planetary-waves, forced-vs-internal-variability]: "Here trends in the meridional extent of atmospheric waves over North America and the North Atlantic are investigated in three reanalyses, and it is demonstrated that previously reported positive trends are likely an artifact of the methodology." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [blocking]: "Moreover, the frequency of blocking occurrence exhibits no significant increase in any season in any of the three reanalyses, further supporting the lack of trends in wave speed and meridional extent." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
