# Quantifying impacts of the 2018 drought on European ecosystems in comparison to 2003

## Summary

本文把 2018 年欧洲"更热干旱"（hotter drought）与 2003 年极端高温干旱对照，量化其对生态系统的影响：用 NCEP/NCAR 500 hPa 位势高度、CRU TS 4.01 最高气温和气候水分平衡（CWB = P − PET）刻画气候驱动，用 231 m、16 天分辨率的 MODIS NDVI/EVI 分位数表征植被响应，并按 CORINE 五类土地覆被（草地、耕地、针叶林、混交林、阔叶林）分层，比较 2003 年 8 月与 2018 年 7 月这两个各自的干旱峰值期。结果显示 2018 年呈"偶极型"干旱格局——中北欧极端干热、地中海偏湿，热浪与干旱峰值比 2003 年提前约一个月；出现最低 NDVI 分位数的面积是 2003 年的 1.5 倍（409 000 km² 对 272 000 km²），CWB 对植被指数的解释力在 2018 年更高（混合效应模型边际 r² = 0.37，加入土地覆被与年份随机斜率后条件 r² = 0.48）。作者把 2018 年更强的耦合归因于干旱中心落在抗旱性较弱的北欧/中欧生态系统，并发现草地与耕地对 CWB 的敏感性高于森林。边界在于：干旱以标准化 CWB 异常度量、气候网格（0.5°）远粗于 MODIS 像元，森林死亡与碳循环的滞后效应只能援引文献推断而未在本设计中实测。

## Limitations & gaps

- Climate grids of 0.5° are far coarser than the 231 m MODIS pixels, so elevation-driven and groundwater-driven variation inside a grid cell is not represented (C20); the authors attribute isolated high-vegetation-index patches inside extreme drought to this mismatch.
- Standardized CWB anomalies can label water-surplus cells as drought-affected; 5 (1) % of grid cells had positive raw CWB despite deficit anomalies, which the authors judge to have generally low impact on the analysis (C21).
- The choice of vegetation index changes what "response" means: NDVI tracks leaf colouration and premature senescence while EVI tracks canopy structure and early leaf shedding (C14), and EVI showed subtle differences of opposite sign in extremely dry regions (C23).
- The explanation for the stronger 2018 coupling—drought arriving earlier over less drought-adapted ecosystems (C16)—is presented as a hypothesis; the authors state it needs support from sensitivity analyses and sub-classification of land cover.
- Forest dieback, forest edges and legacy effects cannot be resolved at 231 m and are inferred from literature and from the 2015 Franconia event rather than measured here; the carbon-budget comparison with 2003 is explicitly flagged as difficult because of the 2018 dipole.
- Impact figures such as the 105 million m³ wood-loss estimate (C19) and the early leaf-shedding reports are external/administrative evidence, not outcomes of the presented analysis.
