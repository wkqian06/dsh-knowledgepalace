# Observed poleward expansion of the Hadley circulation since 1979

## Summary

利用三套再分析与三套 OLR 资料，发现 1979 年以来哈得来环流向极地扩张约 2–4.5 个纬度，再分析显示扩张主要出现在夏秋季，OLR 资料则无此季节性。作者推断亚热带下沉带向极地扩展会使亚热带干旱带随之移动，并可能增加中纬度干旱频率。该推断是作者的展望，不是本文直接检验的结果；仅读到摘要。

## Claims

- C1 [hadley-circulation]: "Using three meteorological reanalyses and three outgoing long-wave radiation (OLR) datasets, we show that the Hadley circulation has a significant expansion of about 2 to 4.5 degrees of latitude since 1979." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [hadley-circulation, drought-research]: "The expansion of the Hadley circulation implies a poleward expansion of the band of subtropical subsidence, leading to enhanced mid-latitude tropospheric warming and poleward shifts of the subtropical dry zone." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C3 [hadley-circulation, drought-research]: "This would contribute to an increased frequency of midlatitude droughts in both hemispheres." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
