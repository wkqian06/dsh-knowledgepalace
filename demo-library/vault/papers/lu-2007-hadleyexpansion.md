# Expansion of the Hadley cell under global warming

## Summary

IPCC AR4 气候变化模拟一致显示哈得来环流减弱并向极地扩张，亚热带干旱带随之向极地扩展。作者用简单尺度分析指出，亚热带静力稳定度增大把斜压不稳定区推向极地，从而决定环流外缘。仅读到摘要，尺度分析的推导与模式间离散度需读全文。

## Claims

- C1 [atmospheric-circulation, hadley-circulation, circulation-response-to-warming]: "A consistent weakening and poleward expansion of the Hadley circulation is diagnosed in the climate change simulations of the IPCC AR4 project." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C2 [atmospheric-circulation, hadley-circulation]: "Associated with this widening is a poleward expansion of the subtropical dry zone." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
- C3 [hadley-circulation]: "The expansion of the Hadley cell is caused by an increase in the subtropical static stability, which pushes poleward the baroclinic instability zone and hence the outer boundary of the Hadley cell." — Abstract / OpenAlex record
