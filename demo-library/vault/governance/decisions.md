## 2026-11-08 — Open drought-research domain

- Seed: Hazaymeh and Hassan (2016), DOI 10.3934/environsci.2016.4.604.
- Scope: drought monitoring, causes, forecasting, agricultural and ecosystem impacts. This literature concerns observed environmental hazards.
- Affected objects: domains.md, concepts.md, and papers assigned to drought-research.

## 2026-11-24 — Open atmospheric-circulation domain

- Seed: Lu, Vecchi and Reichler (2007), DOI 10.1029/2006GL028443 (poleward expansion of the Hadley circulation).
- Scope: large-scale atmospheric circulation and its variability — Hadley cell, planetary waves, blocking, modes of variability, moisture transport, teleconnections. Reasoning: the seed paper asks a circulation-dynamics question and cites and answers that community's literature, not the drought-monitoring literature that drought-research holds. Papers that explain a drought, heat wave or flood through circulation (hu-2007-hadleypoleward, hurrell-1995-naotrends, gimeno-2016-moisturetransport, seager-2007-aridtransition, lau-2012-pakistanrussia) serve a drought-research question and carry both domains.
- Affected objects: domains.md, concepts.md (domain root with five sub-concepts; task and pattern concepts shared across domains), twenty new paper cards (abstract-level reading), papers/INDEX.md and the derived Graph Index (rebuilt).
